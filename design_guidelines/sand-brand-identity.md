# Sand Tech Visual Identity Essentials (V1.0) — Distilled from official brand deck (April 2026)

## The Visual North Star
- Aesthetic: Minimalist, Ultramodern, Authoritative. Swiss-style (Massimo) influence.
- Goal: feel like a high-end technical interface — clean, precise, premium. "Confident Restraint" (less is more).

## Typography (two-font system, do not use other fonts)
- Primary typeface: **Inter Tight** — all headlines, subheads, and body text. Bold for headlines, Regular for body. Generous line spacing (~1.15x for headlines, comfortable for body).
- Technical/data typeface: **Lilex** (monospace) — captions, technical labels, small data points, tags, breadcrumbs. Use sparingly to add a "technical/coded" feel.

## Color palette (dark mode, high contrast)
Primary (monochrome) colors:
- Almost Black `#0a0a0a` — main background
- Oil `#0f0f0f` — secondary surface
- Onyx `#202020` — cards / elevated surfaces
- Gravel `#4d4d4d` — muted text, hairline divider lines, borders
- White `#ffffff` — headlines / primary text on dark

Accent colors (use sparingly):
- Cadet Grey `#80a2b4` — primary actions (buttons, active menu links, primary next steps)
- Coral Red `#f54343` — the one "key number" in a chart, critical alerts
- Squash `#f1ae0a` — data highlights, technical markers

## Color application: the 90/10 rule
- 90% of the brand is monochrome (black, white, grey); 10% reserved for accents.
- An accent is a visual "signal" — draws the eye to the single most important item on a screen: a CTA, a critical data point, or a status indicator.
- Example pattern: Almost Black background, white headline, a single Cadet Grey button with white text — the eye goes straight to the action because it is the only "color" on screen.
- Technical markers: small 1px lines, corner brackets, or Lilex tags in Gravel or Squash.

## Layout & grid ("Swiss Style Grid")
- Strict left-aligned grid. Avoid centering text.
- Use 0.5–1px hairline divider lines in Gravel (#4d4d4d) to separate content sections.
- Photography: high-contrast, desaturated imagery. Avoid bright "stock-looking" photos.
- The "Frame": generous internal margins on all sides — content floats in a focused safe zone, never touching screen edges. Breathing room is mandatory.
- Long text constrained to a comfortable reading width (roughly the left half/6-of-12 columns on wide screens), not stretched full-width.
- Footers/metadata: small (~10pt) muted Lilex labels in Gravel.
